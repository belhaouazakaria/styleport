# Phase 14L: market discovery, source-vetted gap research

**State:** review branch, not deployed. Stacked on Phase 14K PR #113, which itself is not merged. No production migration, no scheduled market GET, no AI action and no publication authorization.

## What was built

- `lib/growth/market-research/pinterest-trends.ts`: real official Pinterest Trends v5 source (`GET /trends/keywords/US/top/growing?limit=20`). Uses the existing account OAuth token and shared global Pinterest GET admission. Two independent default-OFF environment switches `GROWTH_MARKET_RESEARCH_ENABLED=true` and `GROWTH_PINTEREST_TRENDS_ACCESS_APPROVED=true` are required before any Trends request. A distinct PostgreSQL-locked once-per-rolling-24-hour reservation is persisted before the provider request. Quota uncertainty or provider failure blocks. Pinterest's official Trends API is limited to agencies, Enterprise clients and partners: **do not assume the existing account is eligible**. A Trends 403 returns `API_ACCESS_DENIED` without marking the otherwise healthy Pinterest account as requiring OAuth reauthorization.
- `lib/growth/market-research/gaps.ts`: normalizes actual provider keywords, rejects stale (>48h), unrelated, invalid or prompt-injection-looking inputs, checks all stored (including inactive/archived) Translator and Ideas content for likely duplicates, scores relevant topic gaps, and assigns editorial intent to SayTwist, SayTwist Ideas or SayTwist Playground. Evidence scores are **research heuristics**, not proof of conversions, SEO opportunity, or publication quality. At most twelve preview candidates and six compact activity summary rows retained per scan. All opportunities are explicitly `PROPOSED_FOR_EDITORIAL_REVIEW` and must be separately qualified through the existing content and Pin creation pipelines.
- `lib/growth/market-research/cadence.ts`: pure proactive research need decision. Research can be due immediately on content opportunity shortfall after the 24h cooldown, and is independently due every 72h even when internal backlog is full. Does **not** modify the existing three-job local systemd intelligence cycle.
- `POST /api/admin/growth/market-research`: strict ADMIN, same-origin, empty-JSON request. Calls official research only with both opt-ins. No user-supplied URLs/queries/account IDs, no scrapers, no payment, no AI, no content or Pin publishing. Provider error messages and tokens are never shown.
- `/admin/growth/market-research`: view recent market research archives and launch one source-approved manual scan. A dedicated dashboard entry was added. Historical logs contain limited topic descriptions and summary evidence only.

## Release & constraints

These components are **new feature code, not active automation**. No new market source call, content creation, translator publication or Pin approval is authorized. Market research source access has not been confirmed. The scheduler is not connected; enabling external research is a separate owner approval and rate-limit review, and daily cadence remains unchanged until a protected release.

The initial role-aware topic classifier is intentionally conservative: it rejects irrelevant trends, but cannot discover every emerging niche. Further authorized sources (commercial search trend provider / verified market API) should be added behind the same provenance, licensing, quota and budget contracts, not by scraping Pinterest or copying competitor creative. Future integration must persist source evidence and qualified novelty into the bounded daily opportunity portfolio, then route through account-specific Creative Lab and Translator/Idea creation gates, including $2/day/$30/month AI constraints and individual Pin approval. A newly created Translator requires its own reviewed public-activation gate.

Do not merge PR #114 directly into `main`. Its base is the feature branch of PR #113, and production has three SHA-pinned timers. Only a separately approved, protected release with independent backup, PostgreSQL preflight, supported source access, and coordinated timer rotations may activate or deploy it.
